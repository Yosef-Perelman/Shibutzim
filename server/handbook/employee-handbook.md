# Shibutzim Employee Handbook

This is the policy of the company for every hourly employee. It is an invented document for the Shibutzim demo.
Questions that this handbook does not answer go to your manager.

## Overtime

Overtime is counted per work day, never per week. All the time you worked on one work day is added up first, and
then the day's hours are paid in tiers:

- Hours 1 to 8 of the day are paid at 100% of your hourly rate.
- Hours 9 and 10 are paid at 125%.
- Hour 11 and every hour after it are paid at 150%.

A split shift counts as one day: 4 hours in the morning and 6 hours in the evening make 10 hours, so 8 are paid at
100% and 2 at 125%. A shift that crosses midnight belongs to the work day on which it started. There is no weekly
overtime at this time. Overtime beyond 10 hours in a day needs your manager's approval in advance.

## Breaks

You are entitled to a 30-minute break on any shift longer than 6 hours, taken in the middle of the shift and not at
its start or end. Breaks are not deducted from your recorded hours, so you do not clock out for a break. Tell the
person in charge of the shift when you take it so the floor is never left uncovered.

## Sick days

If you are sick, tell your manager as early as you can and at least 2 hours before your shift starts, by phone and
not only by message. After three or more sick days in a row, bring a doctor's note. A shift you miss because of
illness is not paid, but it is never counted against you when the manager builds the next schedule. Return to work
only when you are well enough to do the job safely.

## Shift swaps

Employees cannot swap shifts with each other on their own. To change a shift, ask your manager. The manager can
replace one person with another on the schedule, even after it was published, and the change is visible to everyone
at once. A swap is only approved when the replacement is available for the shift, keeps the minimum rest between
their shifts and stays within 5 shifts for the week. Ask at least 48 hours ahead whenever possible.

## Lateness

Arriving more than 10 minutes after the start of your shift counts as late. Message your manager as soon as you know
you will be late. Time is paid from the moment you clock in, so lateness is never paid. Repeated lateness, meaning
three times in one calendar month, leads to a talk with your manager. Clocking in for a colleague is not allowed
and is treated as a serious breach.

## Availability

Every week you tell the schedule which shifts of next week you can work. For each shift choose available,
preferred or unavailable. A shift you do not answer counts as unavailable, so say yes to the shifts you want.
Preferred shifts are given to you first whenever the schedule allows it. You can change your answers until the
manager generates the schedule for that week. After that, ask your manager to change them. A week that is over can
no longer be changed.

## Minimum rest

There must be at least 8 hours between the end of one of your shifts and the start of the next. The schedule never
puts two shifts closer together than this, and a manager cannot add you to a shift that breaks the rule. You also
work no more than 5 shifts in a week and never two shifts that overlap. If you need more rest, tell your manager.

## Clocking in and out

Clock in when your shift starts and clock out when it ends, with the app. You can have only one open entry at a
time, so clock out before you clock in again. Working a split shift means two entries on the same day. An entry
cannot be longer than 24 hours. If you forget to clock out, do not wait: tell your manager the same day so the
entry can be fixed. Your pay is calculated from your clocked time and not from the schedule, so a planned shift you
did not clock in for is not paid.

## Corrections

If an entry is wrong, because you forgot to clock in or out or clocked at the wrong time, ask your manager to
correct it. Only managers can add or change a time entry. Each correction records who made it, and entries made by
hand are marked as manual. Report mistakes in the month they happen, because after the pay run the correction is
paid in the next month.

## Pay day

The pay period is the calendar month. Your hours for the month are paid on the 9th of the next month. When the 9th
falls on a weekend, the payment arrives on the working day before it. You can see your hours, the tier of each hour
and the amount for every work day in the pay screen of the app at any time during the month. The company deducts no
tax or other amounts in the app: these are handled by payroll.
