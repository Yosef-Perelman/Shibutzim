For managers. Returns the hours every employee worked in a date range from the clocked time entries: per person the
hours, the overtime hours and the entries still open, plus the team total. People with no hours are left out. Use it
for questions such as "who worked the most this week?" or "how many hours did the team do in March?". Pass `from` and
`to` as YYYY-MM-DD dates; both days are included. Never add the numbers yourself: quote the values returned.
