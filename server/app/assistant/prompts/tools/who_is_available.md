For managers. Shows who answered what for one shift: the names of the employees who marked it preferred, available
or unavailable, with the shift's date, name and time. Use it for questions such as "who can work Tuesday morning?".
Pass the shift's `day` (YYYY-MM-DD) and, when that day has more than one shift, part of its name in `shift`, such
as "morning"; or pass the `shift_id` if you know it. People who gave no answer are not listed: for the scheduler a
missing answer counts as unavailable.
