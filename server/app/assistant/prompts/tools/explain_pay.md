Explains the pay of the current employee in a date range: for each work day the hours, the amount, and each overtime
tier with its rate (100, 125 or 150 percent), hours and amount, plus the totals and the hourly rate. Use it for
questions such as "why is my pay lower than last week?" or "how is my overtime paid?": call it once for each period
you need to compare and explain the difference from the returned numbers. Pass `from` and `to` as YYYY-MM-DD dates;
both days are included. The amounts are in Israeli shekels (ILS). Never calculate pay yourself; quote the values.
