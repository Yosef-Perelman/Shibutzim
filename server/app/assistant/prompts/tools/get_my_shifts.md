Lists the shifts the current employee is planned for, in a date range, from the published schedules. Use it for
questions such as "when do I work tomorrow?" or "what are my shifts next week?". Each shift has its date, weekday,
name, start and end time in local time, and whether it ends the next day. Pass `from` and `to` as YYYY-MM-DD dates;
both days are included. A week starts on Sunday. Shifts of a schedule the manager has not published yet are not
shown, so an empty list can mean "not published yet" as well as "no shifts".
